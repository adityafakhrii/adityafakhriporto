## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-contrast minimalist editorial aesthetic tailored for a technical mentor, public speaker, and fullstack engineer. It fuses the quiet confidence of high-fashion print layouts with technical precision and modern digital product design. 

The visual personality relies on dramatic asymmetry: deep, saturated botanical darks juxtaposed with immaculate, clinical white surfaces. This tension creates an elevated atmosphere where craftsmanship and technical authority are immediately evident. 

### Core Tenets
- **High-End Architectural Contrast:** Broad planar shifts between dark emerald green and crisp alabaster/white cards.
- **Editorial Typography Pairing:** Monumental condensed grotesque display typography combined with high-contrast serifs for personal touches, grounded by neutral geometric sans for dense technical reading.
- **Strict Restraint (Zero Emojis):** Expression is conveyed strictly through typographic proportion, numeric callouts, fine directional glyphs (`↗`), and architectural line work.
- **Micro-Detailing:** Precision 1px hairline dividers, subtle translucent glass borders, and atmospheric radial illumination rather than heavy dropshadows.

## Colors
The system is built on a split foundation: nocturnal luxury and crisp daylight. The primary palette drives focus between immersive brand moments and high-legibility project documentation.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#022C22` - Deep Luxury Emerald):** Serves as the deep chromatic foundation for the hero canvas left panel, primary high-priority buttons on light surfaces, and dark narrative accent blocks.
- **Secondary (`#064E3B` - Refined Emerald):** Used for subtle radial glow lighting, nested badge backgrounds, and interactive hover states on deep emerald backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Emerald Accent):** Reserved for technical indicators (availability markers, live status beacons, inline technical highlights, and interactive text states).
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Black):** The primary text color across all white and alabaster surfaces, providing high contrast without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Supporting Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** Dominates the right side hero card, main page body, primary modal cards, and contrasting CTA pill elements.
- **Clean Alabaster (`#F8FAFC`):** Micro-containers, project preview cards, testimonial insets, and technical data grids.
- **Crisp Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline structure throughout the interface; keeps layouts razor-sharp without heavy visual weight.
- **Muted Slate (`#475569`):** Secondary descriptions, meta attributes, timestamps, and architectural labels.

## Typography
The typography system uses a strategic tri-font hierarchy to communicate scale, authority, and technological clarity.

### Font Hierarchy & Roles
- **Bebas Neue (Display & Metrics):** Employed strictly for all-caps display moments—the hero "CREATE" anchor and bold numerical impact statistics (`5,000+`, `93%`). Its dense verticality evokes editorial magazine covers.
- **Playfair Display (Editorial Nuance):** Used selectively for brand signatures (the top-left "Aditya" wordmark), oversized quotation marks (`“`), and testimonial quotes. This softens the technological edge with personal human elegance.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface & Body):** The workhorse typeface for navigation, body text, subheadings, and micro-labels. Its modern geometric structure and tall x-height maintain high legibility across small code snippets, impact details, and project notes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a split-screen viewport architecture that transitions into a disciplined 12-column editorial grid.

### Split-Screen Composition (Hero)
- **Desktop (1024px+):**
  - Left Canvas (52% width): Deep Luxury Emerald background with a central soft radial vignette (`radial-gradient(circle at 60% 50%, #064E3B 0%, #022C22 75%)`). Houses the signature monogram, the giant `CREATE` display typography, social proof clusters, and primary CTA.
  - Right Canvas (48% width): Elevated pure white panel anchored with generous margins (`1.5rem` from canvas edges) and large corner curvature. Houses top navigation, status badges, core capability highlights, and testimonial cards.
  - Centerpiece Interlock: The transparent subject cutout sits absolute along the baseline, intersecting the vertical divide between the emerald background and the white card.
- **Mobile & Small Tablet (< 1024px):**
  - Stacks vertically into a sequential stream: Emerald hero presentation top (containing brand, headline, and portrait cutout) flowing into white card modules below with standard `margin` padding.

### Grid & Spacing Rhythm
- **Impact Metrics Bar:** Formatted as an edge-to-edge 4-column balanced shelf divided by crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` vertical rules on desktop, collapsing to a 2x2 grid on mobile.
- **Project Catalog:** Alternating 2-column or 3-column asymmetric layout with structured metadata headers (`01`, `AI Platform`, `2024`).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this design system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Depth is achieved via tonal contrast, boundary lines, and controlled light dispersion.

### Depth Strategies
- **Atmospheric Glow:** On dark emerald surfaces, depth is established via subtle, low-opacity radial glows (`#064E3B` and `#059669` at 15–25% opacity) placed behind focal points and primary CTA buttons.
- **Crisp Structural Borders:** Content cards, input fields, and metric shelves rely on hairline strokes (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) to establish shape, completely eliminating blurred shadows on white backgrounds.
- **Floating Island Elevation:** Floating cards (e.g., the right hero panel and quote overlays) use a high-refinement shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.08), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6);`
- **Glassmorphic Sub-layers:** Navigational capsules and floating action buttons over imagery utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` and a subtle `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` perimeter.

## Shapes
The shape language uses deliberate contrast: geometric precision on data grids balanced by smooth, large-radius framing for human elements and containers.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Pill (Full Round / 9999px):** Action triggers, CTAs (`LIHAT KARYA ↗`), status indicators, tag chips, and avatar cutouts.
- **Hero Master Card (`28px` / `rounded-xl`):** The primary white canvas container in the split hero section.
- **Standard Cards & Modals (`16px` / `rounded-lg`):** Project showcase blocks, testimonial modules, and technical experience cards.
- **Micro UI Elements (`8px` / `rounded-md`):** Code stack pills, index tags, and numeric stat badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (On Dark Canvas):** Full-pill shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), `background: #FFFFFF`, `color: #022C22`, font: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, weight: `700`, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.06em`. Accompanied by a clean vector directional arrow (`↗`). Interactive hover induces an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action (On Light Canvas):** Full-pill shape, `background: #022C22`, `color: #FFFFFF`, hairline border `1px solid transparent`. Hover shifts background to `#064E3B`.
- **Ghost / Outline Button:** Hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `background: transparent`, `color: #0F172A`. On hover: `background: #F8FAFC`, `border-color: #059669`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Live Status Badge:** Pill shape, inline flex, padding `4px 12px`. `background: rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.25)`, `color: #059669`. Prepended with an animated 6px solid emerald beacon dot.
- **Tech Stack Tag:** Micro container, `background: #F8FAFC`, `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, `border-radius: 6px`, text: `12px`, weight `600`, `color: #475569`.

### Project Showcase Cards
- **Structure:** Clean alabaster (`#F8FAFC`) body wrapped in a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Header:** Monospaced or condensed numeric marker (`01`, `02`) in emerald accent (`#059669`), paired with a bold category label in uppercase (`AI PLATFORM`).
- **Body:** Card headline in `#0F172A` (`headline-md`), accompanied by clean, conversational project narrative in `#475569`.
- **Footer:** Row of tech stack tags and an explicit action link (`Lihat Detail ↗`).

### Metrics Display Shelf
- **Container:** Horizontal flex ribbon spanning the container, divided with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Value:** `Bebas Neue`, `56px`, `#022C22`.
- **Description:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `13px`, `#475569`, tracking `0.01em`, multi-line compact.

### Testimonial & Quote Modules
- **Container:** Pure Crisp White `#FFFFFF` or Clean Alabaster `#F8FAFC`, `border-radius: 16px`, padding `space-lg`.
- **Quotation Mark:** Large decorative serif glyph (`“`) rendered in `Playfair Display`, `color: #059669`, opacity `0.4`.
- **Attribution:** Compact horizontal flex: small round avatar/company badge, name in `label-caps` (`#0F172A`), subtitle in `#475569`, right-aligned numeric score chip (`4.9/5`).

### Input Fields & Contact Elements
- **Text Inputs:** `background: #FFFFFF`, `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `14px 18px`, `border-radius: 10px`, text `#0F172A`. Focus state uses `border-color: #059669` with zero shadow ring for clinical cleanliness.
